Primary prevention

Build the prevention layer before the crisis layer.

The long-term product is not only a vault. It is an implementation system for schools, youth sports, campuses, workplaces, clinics, and coalitions: train trusted adults, measure protective factors, route people to human support, and evaluate outcomes.

01

Teach skills early

Healthy relationship skills, consent, social-emotional learning, and bystander action.

02

Train trusted messengers

Coaches, teachers, clinicians, advocates, parents, supervisors, and peer leaders.

03

Create protective environments

Policies, safer reporting options, warm referrals, housing stability, and workplace or school climate.

04

Measure without extracting

Use de-identified prevention metrics while keeping survivor records private and consent governed.

Concept prototype for planning only. It is not medical advice, legal advice, crisis care, or an official service. Research claims require PMID verification before external use; official CDC/DOJ/OVW/WHO sources should be labeled separately.
